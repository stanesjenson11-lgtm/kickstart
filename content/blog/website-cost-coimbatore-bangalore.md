A business website in Coimbatore or Bangalore costs anything from a few thousand rupees to several lakh, and the gap is explained by scope, not by the city. A small static site is at the low end; a custom-designed site with integrations sits in the middle; an online store or web application costs the most. [Hostinger's 2026 guide for India](https://www.hostinger.com/in/tutorials/website-cost) puts a basic 1 to 5 page site at ₹5,000 to ₹50,000 and an agency-built e-commerce site at ₹1,50,000 to ₹4,00,000 or more.

Here is what drives those numbers, so you can tell a fair quote from a padded one.

## What are you actually paying for?

Every website quote bundles the same kinds of work. The total moves with how much of each your site needs.

- **Design.** A template adjusted to your brand is quick. A custom design, drawn for your content and tested on phones and desktops, takes longer and looks like nobody else's.
- **Development.** Turning the design into fast, accessible pages, plus forms, search, booking, payments or anything that talks to another system.
- **Content.** Writing, photography and video. Sites often stall here, so ask who is responsible for it.
- **Search setup.** Page titles, descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and fast load times so search engines can read and rank the site.
- **Content management.** A way for your team to edit pages and publish updates without calling a developer.
- **Launch and support.** Domain, hosting, analytics, testing on real devices and fixes after go-live.

## Template or custom design?

A template is a sensible choice when you need to be online quickly, your content is simple and nobody will judge you on the site itself. It costs less and launches faster.

Custom design earns its cost when the website is part of how customers decide to trust you: a manufacturer selling to export buyers, a studio, a hospital, a premium brand. It also gives you a structure built around your content rather than squeezing your content into someone else's layout. Whichever you choose, make sure you own the domain, the content and the admin login.

## What does a website cost after launch?

The build is a one-time cost. These keep running:

- **Domain name.** Renewed every year.
- **Hosting.** A light static site can be hosted cheaply. Online stores and web apps need more capable hosting and backups. Builders and managed WordPress plans can cost ₹2,000 to ₹6,000 a year at the entry level, per [Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.com/in/tutorials/website-cost).
- **Maintenance.** Security updates, plugin updates, backups and small fixes. Agency retainers for ongoing maintenance commonly run ₹5,000 to ₹20,000 a month, according to [Upgrowth's website cost guide](https://upgrowth.in/website-design-cost-guide-in-india/).
- **Content.** New pages, blog posts, photography and video as the business changes.

Ask every agency which of these are included, for how long, and what happens when the included period ends.

## How do you brief a website so quotes are comparable?

Quotes only compare fairly when every agency is pricing the same scope. Put these in your brief:

1. **The goal.** Enquiries, sales, recruitment, investor confidence.
2. **The page list.** Even a rough sitemap helps.
3. **Features.** Forms, booking, payments, a members area, multiple languages, integrations with your CRM or ERP.
4. **Content.** What you already have, and what needs writing, photographing or filming.
5. **Platform preferences.** WordPress, Shopify, a custom build, or no preference.
6. **Deadline and budget range.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do quotes for the same website vary so much?

Usually because the agencies are not pricing the same thing. One quote may include custom design, copywriting and a year of maintenance; another may be a template with your logo. Compare the scope line by line before comparing totals.

### Is WordPress cheaper than a custom-built site?

Often at the start, because themes and plugins cover common features. Custom builds on frameworks like Next.js can cost more up front but tend to be faster and need fewer plugin updates. The right answer depends on who will edit the site and what it needs to do.

### Does a website include SEO?

A well-built site should include technical SEO: clean structure, fast pages, titles and descriptions, a sitemap and structured data. Ongoing work such as publishing content and earning links from other sites is separate, and it is what moves rankings over time.

### Does it matter whether the agency is in Coimbatore or Bangalore?

Less than it used to. Website projects run well remotely with scheduled calls and live previews. What matters more is whether the team has built sites like yours and can show the results.
